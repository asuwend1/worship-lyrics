Instrumental, "minus one" or karaoke tracks work best when nobody can play.

Music plays in Spotify (the Spotify link above); this app runs a matching clock. Press Start clock at the moment you press play in Spotify.

“Default” follows the default background in the sidebar. “Random” picks a new look each time the song opens.

How to use it · version 1.9.0

1 · Add a song

  1. + New song, then paste the lyrics into the Edit song tab. Sections like Verse, Chorus, Bait and Reff are detected automatically.
  2. Check the song order. Genius usually lists the full order already; fix it if your group sings it differently.
  3. Choose the music: an audio file (best: an instrumental or minus-one track), Spotify (uses a clock) or live musicians.

2 · Teach it the timing once (for recorded music)

  1. On the Live tab press Record timing (S). The music starts from the beginning.
  2. Tap Space at the start of every line. If the recording repeats a chorus, press C; for a section again, R; for an instrumental break, K. Made a mistake? ← undoes the last tap.
  3. Press S at the end. From now on the lyrics move by themselves whenever that track plays. If they feel early or late, nudge with [ ].

Where songs are saved

Best: click Choose songs folder… in the sidebar and pick a folder in Google Drive, Dropbox or iCloud Drive. Each song becomes a plain text file (for example Give Thanks - Don Moen.txt) that anyone can open and edit. Timing, song order and backgrounds go in _worship-app.json, and audio in an audio subfolder. Point the app at the same synced folder on another laptop and everything is there.

To add a song without the app, save a text file in the folder like this (the top lines are optional):

Title: Bersyukur
Artist: ...
Language: Indonesian

Bait 1: … Reff: …

Deleted songs and sessions go to a _trash subfolder, so nothing is lost by accident. The app picks up new and changed files whenever you switch back to its window. Without a folder, songs are saved in this browser only.

Worship sessions

A session is the run-sheet for one meeting: Welcome screen (group name and date), songs, Prayer, Bible reading (reference, optional passage text shown page by page), Sharing (title and name), Announcements and Prayer Requests. Click any item to show it, press N for the next item, or keep pressing Space: after the last line of a song it moves on to the next item. With a songs folder, each session is saved as a text file in a sessions subfolder, for example:

Session: Home Family Caregroup
Date: 2026-11-28

Welcome
Song: Give Thanks - Don Moen
Prayer: Opening prayer | Budi
Bible: Psalm 23:1-3 | WEB
> 1 The LORD is my shepherd…
Sharing: God's faithfulness | Nadya
Announcements:
- Christmas service - Dec 24, 7 PM
Prayer Requests:
- Healing for ...

3 · During worship

  1. Connect the laptop to the TV. Then either click Open TV window and drag it onto the TV (double-click it for full screen), or mirror the screen and press F.
  2. Press Enter to start the music. Any key overrides: during a recording, C or R moves the music too, so you can sing the chorus one more time.
  3. With live musicians (no recording), Space advances, and C 1–9 B R E follow whatever the leader does. A presentation clicker works too (Page Up / Page Down).
