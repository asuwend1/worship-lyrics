# Worship Lyrics: Leader guide

A one-page guide for small-group leaders. The app: **https://asuwend1.github.io/worship-lyrics/**

## One-time setup (5 minutes)
1. **Get access to the shared songs folder.** Ask the group admin to share the Google Drive folder with you, and install *Google Drive for desktop* so the folder appears on your computer.
2. **Open the app in Chrome** (or Edge) and click **Install app** at the top right, so it opens in its own window and works without Wi-Fi.
3. Click **Choose songs folder…** in the sidebar, pick the shared folder, and click **Allow**. All songs and sessions appear.
   - Each time the app is opened, Chrome may ask again: click **Reconnect folder**, then **Allow**.

## Before the meeting
1. In **Worship session**, click **Duplicate** to copy last week's session (or **+ New**).
2. Set the **date** and add songs from the library with **+ Add**.
3. Click the **Sharing**, **Prayer** and **Bible reading** items and fill them in. For the Bible reading: type the reference, click **Open on BibleGateway**, copy the passage there, then click **Paste from clipboard**.
4. Click **Copy run-sheet** and paste it in the group chat, so everyone can practice with the Spotify links.

### Adding a new song
1. **+ New song**, type the title and artist.
2. Click **Search Genius**, copy the lyrics on the Genius page, then click **Paste from clipboard**.
3. Check the **Lyrics check** box in the Edit tab, and fix anything it flags with one click.

## During the meeting
1. Connect the laptop to the TV (HDMI or screen mirroring).
2. Click **Open TV window**, drag it onto the TV and double-click it for full screen, or mirror the screen and press **F**.
3. Click the first item in the session, then:

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| Space, →, Page Down | Next line. At the end of a song, next item |
| ←, Page Up | Previous line |
| ↓ / ↑ | Next / previous section |
| C, 1–9, B | Chorus, verse 1–9, bridge |
| R / E | Repeat this section / jump to the ending |
| N / Shift+N | Next / previous session item |
| Enter | Play / pause the music |
| K / T | Blank screen / title |
| Esc | Leave a text field so the keys work again |

A presentation clicker works too (it sends Page Up / Page Down).

## No one to play guitar?
Give songs an **audio file** (an instrumental or "minus one" track) in **Edit song → Music**. Press **S**, tap **Space** at the start of each line once, and press **S** again. After that, the lyrics follow the music by themselves. Turn on **Hands-free** in the session panel and the music starts automatically, then moves on to the next item when it ends.

## Privacy
The website contains only the app. Songs, names and prayer requests stay in the shared Drive folder. Share that folder only with leaders. Deleted songs and sessions go to the folder's `_trash` subfolder.
